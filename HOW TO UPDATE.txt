HOT CREATION WEBSITE — HOW TO UPDATE
=====================================

ADD A VIDEO
  1. Put the video in its category folder inside VIDEOS:
       01 Realistic & Cinematic
       02 3D & Pixar-Style Animation
       03 CGI & Product Visuals
       04 Explainers
       05 Music & Song Videos
       06 Social Content
       07 Brand & Promo Films
     Any format works (MP4, MOV, HEVC...).
  2. Double-click "Update Website.bat" and wait until it says Done.
  3. Double-click "Preview Website.bat" (or press Ctrl+F5 if it's already open).

REMOVE A VIDEO
  Delete it from VIDEOS, then run "Update Website.bat".

MOVE A VIDEO TO ANOTHER CATEGORY
  Move it to the other folder, then run "Update Website.bat". Its title is kept.

RENAME A VIDEO / CHOOSE THE TOP CAROUSEL VIDEOS
  Open website\media\library.js in any text editor:
    "title"    = name shown on the website
    "featured" = true  -> also plays in the top phone carousel

GOING LIVE
  Upload everything inside the "website" folder to your web host.
  (VIDEOS, Logo and tools stay on your computer.)

FOLDERS
  VIDEOS    your original videos (by category)
  website   the finished website
  Logo      brand logo files
  tools     the update script (no need to open)
